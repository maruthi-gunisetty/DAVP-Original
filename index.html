Academic Project

Data Analysis & Visualization Project (DAVP)

E-Commerce Sales & Customer Behaviour Analysis Using Python

🎯 Project Objectives

  • End-to-end data processing workflow from data acquisition and standardization to EDA.
  • Robust handling of missing values, duplicates, and invalid transaction dates.
  • Statistical breakdown using NumPy and Pandas (measures of central tendency, IQR outliers, correlations).
  • Customer segmentation via RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) rule-based quartile ranking.
  • Multi-library data visualization leveraging Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly interactive graphics.

🔬 Scientific Computing Stack

NumPy:

Vectorized arrays, indexing, random distributions

Pandas:

Series, DataFrames, aggregations, date slicing

Matplotlib / Seaborn:

Statistical heatmaps, boxplots, histograms

Plotly & Web App:

Interactive dashboards, responsive charts, live filters

💡 Strategic Insights & Business Recommendations

1. High-Value Customer Retention

Top customers generate disproportionate revenue with high repeat rates (>90%). Deploy dedicated VIP loyalty incentives to sustain lifetime value.

2. Product Portfolio Mix

Electronics items (Bluetooth Speaker, Wireless Mouse) provide the highest revenue per transaction line. Bundle with lower-ticket accessories to drive AOV.

3. Market Prioritization

The UK forms the dominant market (~48% of sales), followed by Germany and France. Expanding localized marketing in emerging markets like India represents high upside.
