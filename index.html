Academic Project

Data Analysis and Visualization Project

Ecommerce Sales and Customer Behaviour Analysis Using Python

Project Objectives

  • Systematic data processing workflow encompassing ingestion, standardization, and exploratory analysis.
  • Auditing and treatment of missing fields, duplicates, and transaction timestamps.
  • Statistical analysis via NumPy and Pandas including IQR bounds and correlation matrices.
  • Customer segmentation through rule based quartile scoring across Recency, Frequency, and Monetary dimensions.
  • Interactive multi dimensional visualizations utilizing Chart.js, Matplotlib, and Plotly.

Scientific Computing Ecosystem

NumPy:

Vectorized arrays, indexing, random sampling

Pandas:

Series, DataFrames, aggregations, temporal slicing

Matplotlib and Seaborn:

Statistical heatmaps, distributions, boxplots

Plotly and Web Engine:

Dynamic web dashboards, responsive charts, live filters

Strategic Findings and Enterprise Recommendations

1. High Value Customer Retention

Top customers yield disproportionate transaction volume with repeat purchase rates exceeding ninety percent. Structuring targeted VIP loyalty initiatives sustains lifetime value.

2. Product Portfolio Mix

Electronics items such as Bluetooth Speakers and Wireless Mice deliver peak revenue per order line. Strategic bundling with complementary accessories enhances Average Order Value.

3. Market Regionalization

The United Kingdom represents the leading sales geography with approximately forty eight percent of volume. Scaling targeted marketing in emerging territories provides expansion potential.
